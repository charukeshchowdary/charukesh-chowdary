# FitGuide Chatbot

A lightweight chatbot that asks for height, weight, goal, and activity level, calculates BMI, and suggests nutrition plus workout guidance for cutting or bulking.

## Run it locally

Open `index.html` in your browser. No build step required.

## What it does

- Collects height and weight to calculate BMI.
- Asks for your goal (cutting, bulking, maintenance).
- Suggests eating and exercise focus based on your answers.
